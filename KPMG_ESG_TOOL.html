Strategic Pillars and Commitments

  • Integration of ESG: ESG principles are made the “watermark” running through every aspect of KPMG’s operations, advisory, and client engagement in Singapore.
  • Collaboration with Government: Projects like the Sustainable Finance Jobs Transformation Map (JTM) and the Gprnt platform highlight partnerships with the Singapore government to enable sustainable finance and streamline ESG data collection within the financial sector.
  • Corporate Governance: The Corporate Governance Maturity Assessment Tool helps enterprises evaluate and improve governance capabilities to align with Singapore’s evolving regulatory landscape and the International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB) requirements.
